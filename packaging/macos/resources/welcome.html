Bot Farm Silence Tracker

Bot Farm Silence Tracker is a research tool. It looks at public social media accounts and lists the ones that posted regularly and then all went quiet on the same day. That pattern can point to accounts run from the same place. The list is only a starting point for a person to review; it does not prove that any account is a bot.

What this installer does. It installs the Bot Farm Silence Tracker app in the Applications folder inside your home folder, and the bot-farm-silence-tracker command for Terminal in ~/.local/bin. It installs for you only, so it does not need an administrator password.

Choosing platforms. On the next steps, click Customize to choose which platforms to scan. Reddit through the Arctic Shift archive is chosen for you and needs no account. You can change the choice later in the app or with bot-farm-silence-tracker config set-platforms.

Your data stays with you. The program sends nothing anywhere except requests to the public services of the platforms you scan:

  • Reddit archive: arctic-shift.photon-reddit.com
  • Bluesky: public.api.bsky.app (and bsky.social only if you log in)
  • X: api.x.com
  • Reddit official API: www.reddit.com and oauth.reddit.com

There is no tracking and no account with us. Your scans and settings are saved on this Mac only.
